Parameters

Integration. Fourth-order Runge–Kutta with a fixed step h = 0.002 (L/g)1/2. The trail and the four plots use every step, so the motion stays smooth at any speed. In the conservative cases the energy drifts by less than 10−10 MgL over the 100 s of each case.

Units. Parameters in SI units. Values of g other than 9.8 m/s² stand for an effective gravity, such as that of a pendulum moving on a plane inclined at an angle α (geff = g sin α). The dynamics depends only on μ and on the time scale (L/g)1/2.

Chaotic state. With μ = −0.8, γ = 0.25, A = 0.8 and Ω = 2/3 in natural units, the damped and driven pendulum settles on a strange attractor; its card shows the stroboscopic Poincaré section taken once per forcing period.
